Ground rules

  • One glow per screen: gold when it is about reward, ice when it is about the action in front of you.
  • Gold is for what you earned. Blue is for what you are learning. Coral is for a heads-up.
  • The ice behind everything is yours: it fills in as you skate, and recent skills glow brighter.
  • Everything that bounces, sparkles, or draws itself switches off when the device asks for reduced motion.
