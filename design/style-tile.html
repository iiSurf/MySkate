How did it go?

Ground rules

  • One glow per screen: gold when it is about reward, ice when it is about the action in front of you.
  • Skate traces, sparkles, and snow flecks sit behind everything. The glass lets them show through.
  • Gold is for what you earned. Blue is for what you are learning. Coral is for a heads-up.
  • Everything that bounces or sparkles switches off when the device asks for reduced motion.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, with system fallbacks if they cannot.
