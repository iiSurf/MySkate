Ground rules

  • One glow per screen, so it stays calm.
  • Ice-carve lines and slow snow sit behind everything at about 9% strength.
  • Gold means progress (XP, streaks). Rose means stop or caution.
  • Every animation switches off when the device asks for reduced motion.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, with system fallbacks if they cannot.
