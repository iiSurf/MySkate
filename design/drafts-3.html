A competition planner: eight drafts

Your five favorites (11 to 15), turned into a section for planning a skate for the next competition. Layout (11) places the elements on the rink, Timing (13) sets them to the music, the marquee (12) announces the event, Event day (15) plans the day, and Kiss and cry (14) is the result afterwards. All sample data. The event name, scores and times are made up, and the planner never claims what an event requires: your event's rules and your coach decide that.
