Round seven: A, improved

Two takes on draft A, with no Zamboni stripe. Nothing in the app is gone: each Home is shown scrolled all the way down, so you can see every part (your next move, the week, your next ice, tonight's practice list, the competition, blades and all-time hours, level and badges, goals, coach homework, off-ice, friends, recent sessions, and the backup reminder). Plan and Skate are shown too. Same readable typeface, color with a job (blue is learning, gold is earned, coral is caution), and one dark element on each screen: the next move.

A2 · Soft rink

Softer than A: white cards with a soft shadow instead of a navy edge, and a colored strip down the side only where it means something. Your level is a ring around the number. The next move is the one navy card, so the eye goes there first. Your next ice is a ticket again, and the competition countdown keeps its own dark card.
