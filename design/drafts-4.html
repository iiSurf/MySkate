Round six: easy on the eyes

The Zamboni-day colors (ice white, navy, a red accent, a little Zamboni yellow) with one sturdy typeface, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, which was made to be read at a glance. No hairlines, no thin strokes, body text at 17px and up, and the next thing to do always in the same place. Each draft shows Home and the Skate screen. The tabs are Home, Ice times, Plan, Skate, Progress, Profile, each with an icon.

A · Rink board

White cards with sturdy navy edges on pale ice. The first card on Home is always the next move (here: plan tonight's session), with one big button. Below: the week as seven day squares, then four tiles for blade life, all-time hours, level, and badges, each with a bar. On the ice, the clock is huge and the stars are 54px.
